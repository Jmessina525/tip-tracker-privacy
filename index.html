Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Tip Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a utility application for service and beverage professionals. We value your privacy and believe your financial and shift data belongs strictly to you. This Privacy Policy details our data practices.

1. Data Storage & Collection

Tip Tracker operates on a local-first architecture:

  • Shift & Tip Records: Cash tips, credit card tips, hourly wages, hours worked, and tip-out deductions you log are stored locally on your device (via your browser's local storage or device sandbox).
  • Outlet & Settings Preferences: Configured venue names, custom color themes, and tax withholding percentages remain strictly on your device.
  • No Remote Databases: We do not operate remote servers, user account databases, or external profile storage. We do not transmit or store your earnings on outside servers.

2. Data Tracking & Analytics

We do not track you across apps or websites. The App contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party data broker integrations.

3. Third-Party Services & Links

The App does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to any third party. If you access external resources or support links from within the App, their respective terms and privacy policies govern those interactions.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all records remain stored locally on your device, you retain full control over your data. You may delete your recorded history at any time by clearing the App's stored cache/data or by deleting the App from your device.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
